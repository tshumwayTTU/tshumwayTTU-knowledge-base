# Frieren

An elf mage who spent a thousand years barely noticing time passing. She traveled with a hero's party, defeated the demon king, and then watched everyone she journeyed with grow old and die while she stayed the same. She didn't think much about it until Himmel was gone.

## Role in the Story

Frieren is the main character of [[series/frieren | Frieren: Beyond Journey's End]]. After Himmel's death, she sets out on a second journey with her apprentice Fern and the warrior Stark, retracing the route she once took with the hero's party. Little by little, it shows you who she is and how the people she traveled with shaped her without her ever noticing.

## What Makes Her Interesting

She comes across as detached. Her attitude toward mortality in the beginning is pretty flippant, like the lifespans of others aren't something she's had to think about. But the more you watch, the more you see how much she actually absorbed from the people around her. It's subtle. You see it happen as the show goes on.

Her relationship with Himmel is behind everything she does. It comes through even when she doesn't talk about him.
