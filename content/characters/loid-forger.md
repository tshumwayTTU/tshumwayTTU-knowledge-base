# Loid Forger

A spy operating under the codename Twilight. He is composed, calculated, and good at everything. His job requires him to blend in, and he does it well. He put together a fake family to complete a mission and ended up in situations he clearly wasn't prepared for.

## Concept Art

![[assets/loid-concept-art.jpg]]

## Role in the Story

Loid is the main character of [[series/spy-x-family | Spy x Family]]. He adopts Anya and enters a fake marriage with Yor to pull off a mission that requires him to have a family. He doesn't know Anya can read minds or that Yor is an assassin. The show puts him in domestic situations that his spy training doesn't cover, which is most of the comedy.

## What Makes Him Interesting

He is competent at everything except the parts of family life that aren't a mission. The show plays this well. There's a moment where he does something ridiculous as a spy and immediately recovers without breaking character. He adapts fast and keeps the illusion going.

What's underneath that is harder to see, but it's there.
