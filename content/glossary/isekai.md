# Isekai

## Definition

A genre where the main character is transported, reborn, or trapped in another world. Usually a fantasy setting or something that looks like a video game. Has exploded in popularity over the last decade. At this point it is one of the most common genres in seasonal anime, sourced heavily from web novels and light novels.

The main character usually has some advantage the new world doesn't expect. That's the appeal.

## Common Traits

- Protagonist drops into another world from the real one
- Usually overpowered or holding knowledge the world does not have
- World-building is a major part of the story
- Often adapted from web novels or light novels
