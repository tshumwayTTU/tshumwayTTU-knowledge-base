# Arc

## Definition

A self-contained storyline inside a larger series. Each arc has its own setup, conflict, and resolution, but feeds into the bigger picture. Long-running anime rely on arcs to give the story shape. Without them, a 100-episode show is hard to follow. Arcs give you a smaller story to stay focused on while the bigger one plays out.

Arcs also give characters room to grow in ways a single arc or movie cannot.

## Examples

- The Eternity Devil arc in [[reviews/chainsaw-man | Chainsaw Man]] — a group of hunters trapped in a hotel with a devil that regenerates every night. Contained, tense, and a good introduction to what the show does.
- The Sports Festival arc in [[reviews/my-hero-academia | My Hero Academia]] — U.A. students compete in a public tournament while pro heroes scout for sidekicks. One of the stronger early arcs in the series.
