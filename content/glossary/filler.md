# Filler

## Definition

Episodes or arcs not adapted from the source material. When an anime catches up to the manga, the studio needs time before it can keep adapting. Filler buys that time. These episodes are original content written for the anime only. Nothing that happens in filler is meant to affect the main story, which means you can skip it without missing anything.

Filler has a bad reputation because quality varies a lot, and it breaks pacing in series you are already invested in.

## Notes

Shorter seasonal anime avoid filler entirely by design. [[reviews/chainsaw-man | Chainsaw Man]]'s 12-episode first season adapts a specific chunk of the manga with no padding. No filler, no downtime. That's part of why the pacing is solid.
