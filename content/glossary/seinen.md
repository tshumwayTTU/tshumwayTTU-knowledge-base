# Seinen

## Definition

A demographic label for anime and manga aimed at adult male readers. The difference from shonen isn't just age. Seinen tends to go darker, sit with moral ambiguity longer, and take more risks with characters and endings. Things don't always resolve cleanly.

Not every seinen title is grim. Some are comedic or slice of life. What they have in common is that the writing doesn't talk down to you.

## Common Traits

- Mature or dark themes handled without softening
- Morally complex characters without easy answers
- Slower pacing with more atmosphere
- Characters don't always make it
