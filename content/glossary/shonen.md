# Shonen

## Definition

A demographic label, not a genre. Shonen originally referred to manga published in magazines aimed at young male readers. The label stuck and now covers a huge chunk of the most popular anime out there. What ties shonen titles together is not the plot but the themes: growth, rivalry, and bonds between characters.

The protagonist almost always starts weak and gets stronger. Friendships are tested and usually hold. Rivals push the MC harder than any enemy could.

## Common Traits

- MC starts weak and earns their strength over time
- Rival characters who match or exceed the protagonist
- Large ensemble casts with distinct personalities
- Long-running arcs with escalating stakes
