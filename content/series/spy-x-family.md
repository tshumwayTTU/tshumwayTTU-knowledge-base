## Synopsis

A spy needs a fake family to complete a mission. Loid Forger adopts a girl named Anya and enters a sham marriage with Yor, who is secretly an assassin. The premise runs on secrets: none of them know what the others really are.

Except Anya. She is a telepath. She knows everything — about Loid being a spy, about Yor being an assassin — and says nothing. Watching her sit on all of that while playing along is most of the fun. The show doesn't take itself too seriously and keeps both the spy plot and the comedy working at the same time.
