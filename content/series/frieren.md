## Synopsis

An elf mage who outlives everyone she travels with. Frieren spends a decade with a hero's party defeating the demon king, then watches her companions grow old and die while she barely ages. When the hero Himmel dies, she realizes she never really knew him. That regret drives the rest of the story.

She sets out again, retracing the old journey with her new apprentice Fern and the warrior Stark. The show is slow and quiet. It's more about Frieren figuring out who she missed out on knowing than it is about the journey itself.
