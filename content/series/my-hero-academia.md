## Synopsis

Most people in this world are born with a superpower called a Quirk. Professional heroes are a real career. Izuku Midoriya is born without one, which should disqualify him from the whole thing. He ends up inheriting the power of All Might, the greatest hero alive, and gets into U.A. High School — the most prestigious hero training program in the country.

The show follows Izuku and his classmates as they train, compete, and face increasingly serious villain threats. It is a long-running shonen with a big cast. The early seasons are strong. Later seasons get darker and lean harder into an ongoing war arc.
