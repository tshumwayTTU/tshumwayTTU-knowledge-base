## Synopsis

Human fears create devils in this world. The bigger the fear, the stronger the devil. Devil hunters are hired to kill them. Denji is a young hunter drowning in debt, living with his devil dog Pochita just to scrape by. The yakuza kill him. Pochita fuses with him to bring him back, turning him into Chainsaw Man.

A government agency recruits him after that. The show follows Denji trying to live a normal life while hunting increasingly dangerous devils. It's crude, violent, and funny. Characters don't last as long as you'd expect, which keeps it unpredictable.
