## Synopsis

Ten years ago, portals opened. Monster-filled dungeons started appearing and humans with special abilities became hunters who go inside to fight them. Sung Jinwoo starts at the bottom. He is known as the weakest hunter alive, surviving by luck more than anything else.

After a near-fatal dungeon nearly wipes out his whole party, he discovers something no other human has: the ability to level up like a video game character. He is the only one who can do it. The show tracks his climb from absolute zero to the strongest hunter in existence. The show is about watching him get stronger. That's the whole appeal and it works.
