# Emotional Watches

Shows that hit harder than you expect. These aren't sad for the sake of being sad. You end up having to sit with them afterward.

---

## Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

The easiest recommendation on this list. Every episode has at least one moment that lands. The quiet ones hit harder than the fights. It's about grief and the people she took for granted.

Rating: [[reviews/frieren | 10/10]]

---

## Chainsaw Man

It's usually grouped with action shows but the emotional beats are real. Pochita saving Denji at the start sets the tone. You get attached to characters and then the show reminds you that it doesn't owe them a good ending. When Himeno was taken out I didn't see it coming.

Rating: [[reviews/chainsaw-man | 7/10]]
