# Found Family

Shows built around a group of people who end up caring for each other more than they expected.

---

## Spy x Family

The most literal example on this list. Loid, Yor, and Anya are all playing a role, but none of them are pretending as hard as they think they are. It's clear they care for each other even when the majority of them don't believe they're actually a family. Anya knows everything and goes along with it anyway.

Rating: [[reviews/spy-x-family | 8/10]]

---

## Chainsaw Man

Found family, if you squint. Denji, Aki, and Power becoming friends is one of the best parts of the show. They balance each other well. I wished there were more scenes with just the three of them.

Rating: [[reviews/chainsaw-man | 7/10]]

---

## Frieren

Less obvious than the others, but Frieren's new journey with Fern and Stark is a found family story. She spent her whole life not forming attachments. Little by little you see that change.

Rating: [[reviews/frieren | 10/10]]
