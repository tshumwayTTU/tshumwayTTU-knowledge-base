# Easy Watches

Shows that are easy to follow and entertaining without being heavy. Good for when you want something to watch without committing too hard.

---

## Solo Leveling

One goal. One character. He gets stronger every episode. There isn't much substance or personality, but it doesn't need to be. If you want to watch someone go from the weakest to the strongest with good fight scenes along the way, this is it.

Rating: [[reviews/solo-leveling | 8/10]]

---

## Spy x Family

The plot is easy to follow, the comedy lands, and the characters are fun. It doesn't take itself too seriously. Good for when you want something entertaining without it being heavy.

Rating: [[reviews/spy-x-family | 8/10]]
