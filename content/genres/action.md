# Action

## Definition

Centers on physical conflict, combat, and high-stakes situations. One of the most popular genres in anime. The main draw is fight scenes and momentum. Action overlaps heavily with fantasy, superhero, and dark fantasy — most shows in those categories also qualify here. What separates it from other genres is that combat is the primary way conflict gets resolved.

Production quality matters here more than in most genres. A well-animated fight scene can carry a whole episode.

## Traits

- Combat is how conflict is resolved, not a side element
- Fight scenes are a major production priority
- Stakes are high and usually feel real
- Characters often grow stronger as the story goes on
