# Romance

## Definition

Focuses on the development of a romantic relationship. Ranges from lighthearted and comedic to slow-burn and emotionally heavy. Some anime center entirely on it. Others weave it through a bigger story without making it the main event. What defines the genre is that the romantic dynamic between characters is part of what you are watching for.

Most of the interest is in the tension before anything is actually resolved.

## Traits

- Romantic relationship is central or significant to the story
- Emotional tension between characters is a consistent draw
- Overlaps frequently with comedy, drama, or slice of life
- Pacing of the relationship development matters a lot

## Shows in KB

None of the five shows in this KB are primarily romance. But [[reviews/spy-x-family | Spy x Family]] has it built into the premise. Loid and Yor are married for cover with no real feelings behind it — except the dynamic between them is one of the more entertaining parts of the show, and it does start to shift the longer the show goes on.
