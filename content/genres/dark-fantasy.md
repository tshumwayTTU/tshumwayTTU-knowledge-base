# Dark Fantasy

## Definition

Fantasy world-building with a darker tone underneath. Death and loss are taken seriously. Characters are not always heroic. The world does not operate on clean moral rules. It doesn't mean dark for dark's sake, but don't expect things to wrap up cleanly. The world usually doesn't operate on clean moral rules.

## Traits

- Fantasy setting with mature or grim themes
- Death and consequence handled without softening
- Morally complex characters across the board
- Heavier tone, no clean resolutions
