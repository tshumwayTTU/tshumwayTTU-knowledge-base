# Slice of Life

## Definition

Focuses on everyday moments and character relationships. No big villain driving the plot forward. The appeal is in small details: a conversation over dinner, a routine that shifts slightly, how two people start to understand each other. It is a genre that rewards patience. If you go in expecting action, you will be disappointed.

The payoff comes from spending time with the characters, not from things that happen to them.

## Traits

- No major antagonist or external threat pushing the story
- Character relationships are the main focus
- Slower, more deliberate pacing
- Emotional moments land because of ordinary situations, not dramatic ones
